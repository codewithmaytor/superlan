# Superlan website visibility and SEO review

Checked 14 October 2026. Company: Superlan East Africa Limited. Production: https://superlan.co.ke.

This report separates the live website from improvements prepared for review. At the time of this report, production uses commit `54afed5`. The improvements below have passed local checks but are not yet published. Safi Omni Build is a separate company and was not changed.

## What the evidence shows

The website is available and Google has indexed its homepage and one guide. The main current visibility gap is incomplete indexing, followed by limited evidence of completed work and no business listing managed by the approved Google Business Profile account. There is no confirmed site-wide crawl block, domain failure, manual action or Google security issue.

All 13 live sitemap pages returned HTTP 200. Their titles were unique, each had one H1, and canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata were present. The robots file permits crawling and points to the correct sitemap. An invented route returned 404. Checked image and script assets returned 200, with no missing image alt attributes. Decorative and repeated images may correctly have empty alt text.

The technical foundation is present. A good Lighthouse SEO score alone does not measure whether Google has indexed pages, whether content answers a searcher's question, or how a company ranks against competitors.

## Google Business Profile

The approved account's dashboard shows **zero businesses**. It does not manage a Superlan listing. A Google Maps search for the full company name and Nairobi returned other businesses, with no matching Superlan listing. This still does not prove that no listing exists under another name or owner. Ask the directors about existing ownership before creating one, to avoid duplicates.

The next action is to claim or create the correct company-owned listing, if eligible, and complete Google's verification. Directors must confirm the public business name, address or service area, actual hours, phone, website, category, ownership and genuine office photos. Do not invent hours or add keywords to the registered business name. Eligibility depends on serving customers at a physical location or travelling to them. [Google eligibility and representation guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177).

Local results consider relevance, distance and prominence. Complete verified information and genuine reviews help; a profile does not guarantee a ranking position. [Google local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

Vercel documents that `package.json` engines can override the project's selected Node version. The application pins 22.x and was tested on Node 22. [Vercel Node version documentation](https://vercel.com/docs/functions/runtimes/node-js/node-js-versions).

This is a rendering improvement, not a confirmed color failure or a promise of a 100 accessibility score. No colors were changed.

## What has been prepared

Four factual detail pages cover ERP implementation, custom software, managed IT and cybersecurity. Their scope comes from `lib/services-data.ts`, the supplied company-profile facts and the existing delivery guides. No platforms, partner status, support guarantees, client results or reviews have been invented.

The common serializer escapes `<` in JSON-LD.

The homepage hero keeps its existing copy and appearance while declaring the decorative image's loading priority. The client strip keeps its controls and animation behavior while requesting smaller logo images. The only changed existing visible label is “Learn More” to “About Superlan”. New detail pages and link labels add service information. Contact submission logic, mobile menu behavior and dependencies were not changed. No files were deleted.
